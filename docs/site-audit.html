Site audit · 28 August 2026

Amrut Maharashtra

A working review of amrutmaharashtra.org as it stands today — what the portal is, where it breaks, and what it could become. Everything below was checked in the live site.

Stack PHP · Bootstrap Language Marathi (mixed Hindi / English) Reviewed desktop, live production

01 What it is

A positive-news and information portal for AMRUT — महाराष्ट्र संशोधन, उन्नती व प्रशिक्षण प्रबोधिनी, an autonomous body of the Government of Maharashtra.

Who it serves

Economically weaker households in open-category communities that receive no other government corporation's benefit — 24 listed castes, annual income under ₹8 lakh. AMRUT runs offices in all 36 districts.

What the portal does

It is the storytelling arm, not the service arm. Scheme news, beneficiary success stories, district-office activity, plus a broad cultural strand — spirituality, tourism, farming, students, women's achievement.

Two portals, one org

This site is content only. Applications live on a separate portal — mahaamrut.org.in and app.mahaamrut.org.in. The homepage banners deep-link into it; the article pages never do.

What already works

More than there looks to be: working search, comments, per-article view counts, district tagging, a sponsored-content slot, related-articles, share buttons, and a live अमृत परिवार household survey form.

अमृत घडामोडीलाभार्थी स्टोरीब्लॉगअमृत सेवाकार्यदिनविशेषयशस्वी उद्योजकशब्दामृतस्मार्ट शेतकरीसक्षम विद्यार्थीअध्यात्मसामाजिक परिवर्तकस्त्रीशक्तीपर्यटनवार्तालेखशासकीय योजना

03 What it lacks

Fifteen findings, most severe first. Each was reproduced in the browser.

F-01Critical

The visitor counter is fabricating numbers

The footer's "Total Visitors" figure climbs by tens of thousands while you sit on the page, then reads zero on every inner page. On a site whose footer declares "This is an official government website," a counter that visibly invents its own traffic is the fastest way to lose a reader's trust.

Observed on the homepage over roughly one minute:
2,03,935 → 2,60,193 → 2,74,257 → 2,95,354 → 3,09,418
Same footer on about_us.php and any article: 0

F-02Critical

The Contact button does nothing

संपर्क sits in the primary navigation, styled as the one highlighted call to action, and is wired to nothing. There is no contact page anywhere on the site — an email and a phone number in the footer are the whole of it.

Markup: <a href="javascript:void(0);"> with no bound handler. Clicked; nothing happens.

F-03Critical

Every story ends in a dead end

Each article closes with the same block of boilerplate: here is who qualifies, 24 castes, income under ₹8 lakh, now go contact your district office. The site never says where that office is, never links the scheme being described, and never offers a way to apply. A reader who has just been convinced by a success story has nowhere to go.

Example: a beneficiary story on the व्याज परतावा interest-refund scheme prints the Solapur office address inside the prose, then stops. No link to the scheme, no link to the application portal, no district directory on the site.

F-04Critical

A form collecting caste and contact data, with no privacy notice

The अमृत परिवार सर्वेक्षण २०२६ form asks for name, age, gender, caste category, village, district, mobile and email. There is no privacy policy, no consent statement, and no stated purpose or retention period anywhere on the site. Under the DPDP Act 2023 that notice is not optional.

Page: amrut_family_registration.php — served over HTTPS, no linked policy, no consent checkbox.

F-05Major

Search engines cannot see the archive

No robots.txt, no sitemap.xml, and article URLs carry no words at all. Roughly 3,300 Marathi articles — the portal's entire asset — are close to invisible in search, and a shared link tells the recipient nothing.

Both return the 404 page. Article URLs look like news.php?id=3283 rather than a readable slug.

F-06Major

Navigation disappears once you go anywhere

The category bar — the site's whole structure — exists only on the homepage. Open an article, the About page, or a search result and it vanishes, leaving a single "back to home" crumb. Readers cannot move sideways between categories; they can only bounce back to the top and start again.

Confirmed on about_us.php, search.php, and the article template.

F-07Major

Images are being served at up to 18× the size they display

Around 80 images load on the homepage, none lazily, none responsive, effectively none in a modern format. A 1600×2133 photograph is downloaded in full to fill a 150×200 thumbnail. For an audience largely on rural mobile data, this is the single biggest cost the site imposes on its readers.

Homepage: ~80 images · lazy-loaded: 0 · with srcset: 0 · WebP/AVIF: 1
Worst offenders: 1600×2133 → 150×200 · 1600×802 → 120×60 · 396×631 → 22×35

F-08Major

Nothing about the schemes exists as content

There is no scheme directory, no eligibility page, no document checklist, no deadline listing. Eligibility rules live only as a paragraph repeated at the bottom of every article. The शासकीय योजना category carries news about schemes, but the schemes themselves are never described in one place a person can be sent to.

Consequence: the most common real question — "do I qualify, and what do I need?" — has no page to answer it.

F-09Major

None of the accessibility provisions a government site is expected to carry

No skip-to-content link, no text-size controls, no high-contrast mode, no accessibility statement, no screen-reader page. The sixteen scheme banners on the homepage are image-only links with no accessible text. This is the standard GIGW checklist for an Indian government portal, and none of it is present.

16 links to the scheme portal with empty link text and no aria-label.

F-10Major

The 404 page is someone else's template

A wrong URL lands on a stock illustration of a skateboarder above the words "This Page Does Not Exist." No masthead, no navigation, no link home, no Marathi, no emblem — nothing identifying it as a government site at all. It is where every broken and mistyped link ends.

Reproduced at /robots.txt, /sitemap.xml, and any unknown path.

F-11Major

Search finds everything and ranks nothing

It works — 135 results for शेती — but it is a plain substring match. The top result for "farming" was an article about a coconut festival. No relevance order, no filters by category, district or date, and the results page drops the navigation like every other inner page.

Query शेती → 135 results; positions 1 and 3 had no farming content.

F-12Minor

Two URLs serve an identical homepage

news.php with no parameters renders the homepage byte for byte. Duplicate content, split ranking signals, and a second address for the same page.

F-13Minor

One page title, reused everywhere

Article pages get proper titles. Everything else — the About page included — is served as "अमृत महाराष्ट्र - Latest News & Updates" with the same generic English meta description, on a site written almost entirely in Marathi.

Description on every page: "Latest news, government initiatives, and developments in Maharashtra"

F-14Minor

The header eats a quarter of the screen

Three stacked bars — emblem strip, logo strip, category bar — occupy roughly 200px and stay pinned while scrolling. On a laptop that is a quarter of the viewport permanently spent on chrome; on a phone the top of the page is almost entirely header.

F-15Minor

No way to subscribe to anything

A portal publishing several articles a day offers no RSS feed, no newsletter, no WhatsApp channel — despite a floating WhatsApp button in the corner. Every returning reader has to remember to come back on their own.

04 What to add

Ordered by what changes most for the reader, not by what is easiest to build.

Highest impact

A scheme directory

One page per scheme: who qualifies, what it pays, documents needed, deadlines, and a direct link into the application portal. Every article that mentions a scheme links to its page.

Turns 3,300 stories into 3,300 entry points instead of 3,300 dead ends.

Highest impact

District office finder

All 36 districts, searchable, with address, phone and the district manager's name. The addresses already exist — they are buried in article prose, one office at a time.

Every article already tells people to contact their district office. This is the page that answers it.

Highest impact

Am I eligible?

A short guided check — community, income, district, what you want to do — ending in the schemes that actually apply to you, in Marathi, on a phone.

The rules are unusually specific. Most people cannot self-assess from a paragraph of prose.

Structure

Persistent navigation and real category pages

Keep the category bar on every page. Give each of the 16 categories a proper archive with pagination, and add district and date filters to search.

Fixes the dead end at the bottom of every inner page.

Reach

Findable, shareable articles

Readable URLs, a sitemap, robots.txt, per-page titles and descriptions, NewsArticle structured data, and Open Graph cards that render properly in WhatsApp.

WhatsApp is how this content actually travels in Maharashtra. Right now a shared link previews as nothing.

Reach

An image pipeline

Resize on upload, serve WebP, responsive srcset, lazy-load below the fold. Nothing else on this list is felt as immediately on a slow connection.

Roughly a 10× reduction in homepage weight, with no visible change.

Compliance

Privacy policy and consent

A published policy, an explicit consent step on the survey form, a stated purpose and retention period, and a named grievance officer.

Required before that form should keep collecting caste and contact data.

Compliance

Accessibility pass

Skip links, text-size and contrast controls, alt text and labels on the banner links, keyboard focus states, and a published accessibility statement.

The GIGW baseline for a government portal, currently absent entirely.

Retention

Something to subscribe to

A WhatsApp channel first — that is where the audience is — plus RSS and an optional email digest. Weekly, in Marathi.

A daily-publishing portal with no return mechanism is starting from zero every morning.

Trust

Contact page, real 404, honest counter

Wire up संपर्क to an actual page with a form and the head-office details. Rebuild the 404 in the site's own identity with a search box. Either report real analytics or drop the counter.

Three small fixes that stop the site undermining its own credibility.

The one thing worth deciding first

This site is currently a magazine that happens to be run by a government agency. Everything above points the same way: the missing layer is the bridge between a story and the scheme it is about. A reader finishes a piece about a farmer who got a tractor through the interest-refund scheme, is told he qualifies if he earns under ₹8 lakh, and is then left to find AMRUT's district office on his own.

Build the scheme directory, the district finder and the eligibility check, and every article already published becomes a funnel. Nothing else on the list changes the portal's purpose that much.

05 What I need from you

Answers here decide what the new project in ~/Amrut_Maharashtra actually becomes.

  1. Rebuild or repair? Is this a ground-up replacement of the PHP site, or a new front end on the same content, or a separate section that sits alongside it?

  2. Who owns the content? Is there a CMS and admin panel behind this that we keep, and can we get at the article database and the district office list?

  3. Is the scheme portal ours too? mahaamrut.org.in and the application portal are separate systems. Do we integrate, or just link out well?

  4. Who signs off? An official government portal has an approving authority. Knowing who reviews this changes how much we can move.

  5. Marathi first, or bilingual? There is Hindi and English content mixed in today with no language switch. Worth settling before any content work.

Scope of this review

Checked in the live site on desktop: homepage, About, an article page, search results, the family survey form, the 404, robots.txt and sitemap.xml, plus the page's own markup and image loading. Every finding above was reproduced directly.

Not covered: mobile rendering on a real device, the admin or CMS side, server configuration and security, the separate scheme portal at app.mahaamrut.org.in, and content accuracy. A privacy policy may exist somewhere unlinked — I could not find one from any page on the site.
